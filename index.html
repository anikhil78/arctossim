Apiary Robotics Robotic Laser Surface Prep for Steel Fabrication
What We Do

Apiary Robotics is building autonomous mobile robots that use high-power lasers to remove rust and prepare steel surfaces in structural steel fabrication shops. Our robots replace manual grinding, needlegunning, and inflexible shotblasting systems with a faster, cleaner, and more consistent approach to surface preparation.

Surface Prep Today

Structural steel arrives at fabrication shops covered in mill scale, rust, and contaminants that must be removed before coating. Today's surface prep methods each carry significant drawbacks that drive up costs, slow production, and create hazardous working conditions:

Labor-Intensive Manual Methods

Manual grinding and needlegunning are slow, physically demanding, and heavily dependent on operator skill. Shops struggle to hire and retain workers for this grueling work, creating a persistent labor bottleneck.

Inconsistent Surface Quality

Hand-held tools produce variable results depending on operator technique, fatigue, and time pressure. Inconsistent surface profiles lead to coating failures, rework, and warranty claims downstream.

Health and Safety Hazards

Grinding and needlegunning generate metal dust, noise, and vibration that cause long-term health issues including hearing loss, respiratory disease, and hand-arm vibration syndrome. These are among the most injury-prone tasks in a fab shop.

Shotblasting Limitations

Automated shotblast lines offer throughput but are limited to simple shapes that fit the machine. They require major capital investment, consume large amounts of abrasive media, and generate significant waste that must be managed and disposed of.

Our Approach

Mobile Factory Robot

Our robot moves autonomously around the fabrication shop floor, navigating to workpieces rather than requiring steel to be fed through a fixed machine. A 6-axis arm reaches around complex geometries to prep surfaces that shotblast lines simply cannot handle.

High-Power Laser Cleaning

Our laser precisely ablates rust, mill scale, and contaminants down to bare metal without damaging the steel substrate. No abrasive media, no dust clouds, no spent grit to dispose of — just clean steel ready for coating.

What Sets Us Apart

Flexible, Not Fixed

Unlike shotblast lines that require steel to conform to the machine, our robot conforms to the steel. It handles beams, plates, channels, angles, and complex weldments — adapting to whatever the shop is fabricating that day.

Autonomous Operation

The robot works with minimal human oversight, freeing skilled workers for higher-value tasks. It navigates the shop floor, identifies surfaces to prep, and executes cleaning paths automatically — reducing dependence on hard-to-find labor.

The Impact

Consistent, Repeatable Quality

Laser ablation delivers uniform surface profiles every time, independent of operator skill or fatigue. Consistent prep quality means better coating adhesion, fewer rejects, and reduced rework across every job.

Safer Work Environment

Eliminating manual grinding and needlegunning removes workers from exposure to metal dust, excessive noise, and vibration injuries. The robot handles the most hazardous surface prep tasks so your team doesn't have to.

No Abrasive Waste

Laser cleaning generates no spent media, no dust clouds, and no secondary waste streams. This eliminates abrasive purchasing costs, reduces cleanup time, and simplifies environmental compliance.

Higher Throughput

An autonomous robot that works continuously without breaks, shift changes, or fatigue-related slowdowns keeps your production line moving. Faster surface prep means faster time from raw steel to coated, finished product.
